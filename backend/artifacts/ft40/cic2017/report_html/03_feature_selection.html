03 — Feature selection: top 40 of 77 — CIC-IDS-2017¶

Features are ranked by Random Forest Gini importance fitted on the training side of the stratified 70/30 split only — the test side never influences selection (no leakage). The resulting top-40 set is frozen and reused by every split scheme in notebook 04, so "same features, only the split varies" holds across the whole comparison.

The 70/30 split indices are saved and reused verbatim as the 70_30 scheme.
