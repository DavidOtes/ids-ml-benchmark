03 — Feature selection: top 40 of 77 by Mutual Information — CSE-CIC-IDS2018¶

Features are ranked by their estimated mutual information with the class label (information gain; Kraskov k-NN estimator). A filter method: model-free, and sensitive to non-linear feature-class relationships.

Fitted on the training side of the stratified 70/30 split only — the test side never influences selection (no leakage). The resulting top-40 set is frozen and reused by every split scheme in notebook 04. Selection runs on a class-proportional subsample (SAMPLE_ROWS) of the training portion; rare classes are never dropped.
